FROM VIDEO TO CHANNEL

nothing renders or uploads until the hooks are saved
01
PICK
a model watches the whole match and picks a 12–60 s moment for the Short
no answer? a timeline heuristic picks instead
↓
02
HOOKS
the operator saves the title hook and the Short's hook
left unsaved? auto-hooks saves the best one that passes the spoiler checks
↓
03
SHORT
rendered from the pick's window, or cut by hand (shortsByHand)
no Short for this one? the long-form goes alone
↓
04
LONG-FORM UP
uploads private, scheduled for 19:00 UTC (a series 23:00)
then playlists, the pinned comment, an end screen, a thumbnail test
↓
05
SHORT UP
18 h after the long-form, linked to it
by default. an experiment can move it
