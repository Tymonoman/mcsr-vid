## 5. Risks

- **Partner Program, reused content**: the review looks at the whole channel. *Mitigation*: our own text carries ~85% of the runtime, clips stay ≤15 s each and ≤30 s total, and the video has a clear point of view (a sourced explainer). It's one video among many original replays with overlays.
- **Ad suitability**: a video about hate speech will likely get limited ads, as you expected. *Mitigation*: tick the honest box in the self-certification. Don't show or say any slur, and keep the tone news-like. That keeps it at "limited" rather than "none", and it doesn't affect the rest of the channel.
- **Harassment in comments**, against edcr, trans or minority viewers, or each other. *Mitigation*: set this video's comments to "Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review" (Studio → Settings → Community, stricter level) and add slur variants to Blocked words. The pinned comment sets the rules. Consider the same hold on the existing lauveer vs edcr video, where "free edcr" comments already are. Removing comments is your call, never an auto-delete.
- **Unfair to someone who was 15–16**: YouTube's harassment policy and plain fairness both apply. *Mitigation*: say his age at the time and his apology, show only the official decision's facts, and put no mocking images or words in the title or thumbnail.
- **Takedown by a streamer for their clip** (edcr has no clips or VODs, so only Feinberg or infume could claim). *Mitigation*: ask them first (section 4). If one objects, the section works with a quote card from their YouTube video's title and date instead.
- **Facts change** (an appeal, a new statement): *Mitigation*: the "as of 29 Sept 2026" stamp, plus a pinned-comment update if it moves.
- **Wrong date or wording** (X is snippet-only for us): *Mitigation*: nothing marked unverified in section 1 goes on screen until your screenshots confirm it.

## 6. How we'd build it

- **Cards**: one new Remotion composition, `Explainer`, with three card types: headline, timeline beat and quote card (name, date, text, source handle). It uses Monocraft, the palette in `remotion/overlay.source.css` and `<PixelBadge />`, and follows the `Short.tsx` patterns. X screenshots go in as images inside the quote card frame. It lives on its own branch and doesn't touch the match pipeline.
- **Clips**: download with `yt-dlp https://clips.twitch.tv/<id>` (installed), cut with ffmpeg to the second you choose, and place them in a branded frame with a name and date lower-third.
- **Background**: dimmed seconds from our own `series-13303619.mp4` (R16), muted.
- **Assembly**: render the cards, then join cards and clips with ffmpeg to 1080p60 at the channel's usual export settings. A still check comes first (`npm run still`).
- **Upload**: by hand in Studio as private, then you schedule it. It isn't a match, so the dashboard chain and pairing don't apply, and the API calls stay exactly as the audit recording showed.
- **Time**: about 3–4 h of work once you send the screenshots and approve this outline, plus minutes to render. Then one review round with you on a private upload or the MP4. Nothing is rendered or uploaded before you approve the outline and pick a title.
