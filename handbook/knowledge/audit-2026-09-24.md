## Five things that decide the YPP date

1. **Subscribers per view are falling as reach grows.** Subs per 1k views by week: 4.3 → 2.5 → **0.96**
   (per 1k engaged views: 11.0 → 5.3 → 3.0). Shorts: 0 from 3,197 views. At 1.6–2.1 net a day,
   500 subscribers lands **Apr–Jun 2027**; at ~4.1 a day it lands around **Jan 2027**. Watch hours are
   not the constraint (767 / 3,000 h, due Nov–Dec 2026).
2. **62% of long-form "views" are not real starts.** engagedViews = 38% of views (11,038 of 28,820 in
   28 days), and minutes watched match engaged views, not views. The gap sits entirely on Browse and
   Search (29% engaged) — sources that auto-play muted previews — while Suggested is 90% engaged. The real
   audience is ~11k starts a month. (Interpretation of the gap is unverified against Google's docs; the
   arithmetic is not.)
3. **The first minute loses 33–49 points of every start, and half of it before the race begins.**
   Median loss 22.5 points in 0–3% (the 7 s intro card and the frozen 10 s countdown), then 21.2 points in
   3–10% (spawn and the first 40 s). Relative retention at 0% is below the median on 11 of 14 videos.
4. **The finish is the most-watched moment, and nothing follows it.** 12 of 14 curves lift at the end
   (viewers skip to the finish); the post-roll then drops 3–28 points with nothing to click. No long-form
   has an end screen; cards have 0 impressions ever.
5. **Borrowed audiences are the cheapest lever and have never been pulled.** 12 shares in 90 days
   (0.38 per 1k views). The one video a runner's audience found (edcr vs doogile) brought 26 of the 49
   long-form subscribers. The kit drafts a DM per player on every upload; none has been sent. Feinberg has
   251k subscribers, doogile 117k.

  report (~25–26 Sept): few impressions = Browse never tested it (length, slot, same-day collision);
  normal impressions + low CTR = packaging. Proposal: finish the four joined series, then render more only
  if they average 300+ views at day 7. "SWEPT" in a best-of-5 title reads as a result — retire it.

  nothing; @mcsrmatches' best non-spoiling hooks are plain stakes ("WHAT A MATCH", "SUB 7"). The agy hook
  proposals can offer three registers (nickname pair, stakes line, split-time line) — say if you want that.
