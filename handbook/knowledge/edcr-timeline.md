Compiled 29 Sept 2026. Read-only web research (WebSearch/WebFetch; X itself returns 402/451 to

## 1. MCSR Ranked's official posts (X, @MCSR_Ranked)

- **~15 Sept 2026** (undated exactly in search results — inferred from edcr's own statement date,
  see §2) — an X account (@gjunc53255565, "stizz") posted: "BREAKING: SEASON 10 PLAYOFFS CHAMPION
  AND #1 RANKED PLAYER EDCR EXPOSED AFTER DISTURBING CHAT LOGS SURFACE ONLINE... MCSR OFFICIALS
  HAVE CONFIRMED A FULL INVESTIGATION IS NOW UNDERWAY." — NOT an official MCSR Ranked post, a
  third party's. https://x.com/gjunc53255565/status/2099413052139667699 (UNVERIFIED — snippet
  only, this is not the official account)
- **A third-party account** (@aasfdfghlrgi, "based mcsr") posted "JUST IN: EDCR HAS BEEN BANNED
  FROM PLAYOFFS. HE IS NOT ON THE PHASE POINT LEADERBOARD." — again not the official account.
  https://x.com/aasfdfghlrgi/status/2100731187647885445 (UNVERIFIED)
- **The one confirmed official @MCSR_Ranked post found** — the final decision, dated by its
  content (posted after S11 Playoffs concluded, referenced by Ludwig's reply which is itself
  dated in the days after): "Following the disqualification of edcr from Season 11 Playoffs due
  to past harmful messages which breached our player conduct rules, the Ranked team has reached a
  final decision on his eligibility for future Playoffs. When the messages were first brought to
  our attention we veri[fied it was him. We contacted him the same day; he denied sending them.
  Two days later he posted a public statement taking accountability, directly conflicting with
  what he'd told us. After playoffs concluded we contacted him again; he briefly apologised and
  admitted he'd been dishonest with us initially. He only engaged when directly prompted, never
  reached out on his own.] Final decision: permanently banned from future Playoffs."
  https://x.com/MCSR_Ranked/status/2103194125361467657 — this is the fullest text recoverable
  from search snippets (WebFetch on the URL itself returns HTTP 402). CONTENT VERIFIED across
  three independent search queries returning the identical snippet; the *page itself* was not
  opened.
- An earlier @MCSR_Ranked post exists asking fans "Ever had a question you wanted to ask edcr?...
  may get answered in a upcoming content piece for S11 Playoffs" — predates the scandal,
  shows he was being promoted as a Playoffs storyline right before it broke.
  https://x.com/MCSR_Ranked/status/2095624865285841107
- GAP: the *disqualification* announcement itself (distinct from the later final-decision post)
  was not separately recovered — only referenced inside the final-decision post's own text
  ("Following the disqualification of edcr from Season 11 Playoffs...").
- Liquipedia's Season 11 Playoffs bracket page confirms edcr's quarterfinal vs Feinberg is

## 2. edcr's own public statement

- Mirrored on Pastebin (a copy/repost, not the original platform — original was likely
  TwitLonger or X given the "posted a public statement" phrasing in MCSR Ranked's post):
  https://pastebin.com/NQ1M6wUr
- Dated by the pastebin analysis to **15 Sept 2026**.
- Content, neutrally: he says that "back when [he] was 15 and 16" he said hateful things
  including slurs (racial and transphobic, per MCSR Ranked's characterization) in "the zombies
  community" [a separate gaming community, not MCSR]; says he was "trying to fit in" when school
  and home didn't offer belonging; says the MCSR community "had a huge impact on my life and has
  made me into a much better person"; says he is "19 now" and has "lived with this throughout the
  duration of my mcsr career"; closes "i'm sorry to everyone that i hurt."
- Per MCSR Ranked's own later post (§1), this statement directly conflicted with what he had told
  them privately (he'd denied sending the messages when first contacted, then took public
  accountability two days later) — MCSR Ranked's own account calls this out as a discrepancy.
  UNVERIFIED beyond the pastebin mirror; the original post location was not located directly.

## 3. What other runners / community figures said publicly

- **Ludwig (LudwigAhgren)**, X: "this certainly isn't an easy decision to make and there's little
  reward to being a governing body of a niche speedrun comunity- but the penalty of a permanent
  ban leaves little room for change/growth. I hope the community can persist so that there are
  still playoffs happening 5 or 10 [years from now]..." — reads as measured criticism of the
  permanence of the ban, not support for edcr's conduct. https://x.com/LudwigAhgren/status/2103215779613462644
  (snippet only, page not opened — 402 blocked)
- **pickle (@p1ckle03)**, X, laid out the timeline as understood by fans: "So the timeline for
  this is something like, his messages surfaced. He was contac[t]ed by ranked team. Denied it.
  Got banned from playoffs s11. Released a statement. Got contacted by the team again and then
  told the truth." https://x.com/p1ckle03/status/2103207111681573118
- **skye (@skyewayy)**, X: "Edcrs past was very hurtful and lying about it was bad and a
  punishment is valid. Also, a permanent ban heavily discourages improvement in a person. Both
  can be true. In my opinion, an improvement in conduct should warrant an ability to appeal the
  ban after a[n] year for everyone" — an appeal-path position, distinct from "free edcr."
  https://x.com/skyewayy/status/2103250364002914760
- Two named top players made their own YouTube videos specifically about it (see §5): **Feinberg**
  ("Feinberg Addresses The Edcr Situation....." — Feinberg was edcr's walkover opponent) and
  **Infume** ("Infume Talks about the edcr Situation...."). Neither video's content was read in
