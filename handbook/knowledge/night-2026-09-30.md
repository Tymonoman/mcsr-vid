## The post-mortem of every upload (handbook/knowledge/uploads-postmortem-2026-09-30.md)

Seven analysts over 53 videos (Analytics to 28 Sept); every headline number recomputed. What we did
wrong, ranked by cost:

1. Empty days. 11 of the first 21 days of September had no long-form. A launch day brought 3.8
   gross subscribers and 3,575 views; a day without, 2.2 and 2,180. About 15 subscribers and
   13,000–18,000 views lost in September. Fix: queue two deep, hooks in one sitting,
   `nightlyMaxRenders: 2` (yours).
2. The 23:00 slot went to series. Since 14 Sept a ranked launch returned 2.0 subscribers and 3,513
   views; a series 1.2 and 1,046; four of five series drew 600 impressions or fewer on day 0. The
   quarterfinals rendered tonight carry roster names, which is the one case the data allows; a
   series without a name should not take the slot.
3. We steered by views. Views up 10.4x from late August to late September, subscribers 1.5x;
   per 1,000 views 7.0 → 0.98; Browse went from 67% to 90% of views at one minute a view. At the
   28-day pace 1,000 subscribers land 3 Nov 2027; the bot's 7-day projection (21 May 2027) is the
   optimistic bound. 1 Feb 2027 does not bind: 8,000 hours arrive on time at any pace.
4. One event paid for 23% of all subscribers: edcr vs doogile (26 Aug), driven from outside the
   channel. No runner DM was ever sent. The kit's DM now hands each runner their moment; sending
   two or three a week is yours.
5. Nothing sends a viewer onward: end screens brought 34 views lifetime, playlists 42; 32% of
   subscribers have no video behind them (the channel page). Tonight's shelves and trailer, the
   pair's playlist in the description and pinned comment; end screens in Studio are yours.
6. Two weeks of packaging work for effects under the noise floor: the 90 s subscribe line, the
   teaser, the 5 s intro and the new thumbnails show nothing measurable at n=28. Stop iterating
   on stills; compare groups of five or more at a matched engaged share.
7. Shorts: 11,711 views, 4 subscribers; 22% of views, 9% of subscribers, 1.7% of watch minutes.
   Keep them for reach, out of every YPP projection; set the Related video on live Shorts (Studio).
8. Lifetime CTR read as a verdict: ranked uploads run 7.6% on day 0 and 3.4% on day 1, so 58
   MILLISECONDS on 12 hours of data was never a flop. Print the age beside every CTR.
9. "LOVERBOY vs TAS" sits on two videos with different second players; "TAS" has stood for four
   players in seven titles. One nickname per player, a line about the match beside it.
10. TN0_s3y0HH8, a public Short, links the deleted long-form Edj_-c7lK38. Repoint or privatise it
