# Title memes

Weekly scan of what the MCSR / Minecraft speedrunning community is joking about, for bending into
video titles. Newest research on top. Hard rules (see `CLAUDE.md`): never mock, label or nickname
a runner, or reference ethnicity/nationality/looks/age/skill; a meme about a specific runner only
if affectionate and started by that runner, sourced; no result spoilers; must land with a cold
viewer or be widely known in MCSR.

## Week of 18 Oct 2026

- **Pie chart (Pie-Ray / "Piedar")**: the long-running MCSR in-joke/technique of reading the F3
  debug menu's pie chart (Block Entities) to snipe a Nether fortress location from spawner data
  instead of just running blind · source: https://www.speedrun.com/mc/forums/wn4tq ,
  https://www.speedrun.com/mc/forums/54hx0 · title use: "THE PIE CHART CALLED IT" / "PIE CHART
  SNIPE" · fits when: a fast, clean fortress find that clearly came off a pie-chart read (visible
  F3 pull on the VOD).
- **Zero cycle**: blowing the dragon up with beds from an obsidian pillar while it is still flying,
  before its first perch; the flashiest End finish, glorified in highlight reels · source:
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uUV0HAshto0 ("15 of the Greatest Zero Cycle Saves in Minecraft
  Speedrunning") · title use: "ZERO CYCLE OR BUST" · fits when: the run facts list a zero cycle
  (the pick saw it); "ONE CYCLE" puns only when they list a one cycle (beds on the first perch).
- **Chicken Jockey**: the *A Minecraft Movie* (2025) audience-participation scream-meme ("CHICKEN
  JOCKEY!!") — still active in 2026 (a Bedrock Valentine's Day pop-up referencing it on 14 Feb
  2026, a tie-in book released 7 Jul 2026), universally known even outside MCSR · source:
  https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/chicken-jockey-minecraft-movie · title use: "CHICKEN JOCKEY!!" /
  "AN ACTUAL CHICKEN JOCKEY" · fits when: a real chicken-jockey mob (baby zombie riding a chicken,
  ~0.5% spawn) actually appears on either POV — rare and verifiable on the VOD, no spoiler risk.
